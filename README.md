Data and analytics portfolio


Selected Projects:
1. Education & Labor Market Inequalities
Goal
Analyze how differences in education inputs translate into labor market outcomes.
What I did
Worked with large-scale administrative datasets
Built comparative indicators across groups
Analyzed education inputs (resources, teachers, instruction time)
Identified key drivers of performance gaps using quantitative methods
Output Data-driven insights on structural inequalities and their implications for labor market participation.

3. Labor Market Dynamics – Older Workers
Goal
Understand long-term employment trends across age cohorts.
What I did
Cohort and time-series analysis of labor market data
Construction of employment indicators by age, gender, education
Statistical analysis of structural trends over time
Output Evidence-based analysis of aging workforce dynamics and labor market participation patterns.
